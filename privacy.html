Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

What we collect

We collect account details you submit, such as your name and email address. If you connect Instagram through Meta OAuth, we store the account ID, username, encrypted access token, and token expiry needed to provide the integration.

What we never collect

Nudge never asks for, receives, or stores your Instagram password. We do not scrape Instagram or use unofficial APIs.

How we use data

We use data to authenticate you, provide the Nudge service, maintain security, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Integration tokens are encrypted at rest and are not exposed to browser JavaScript. You may disconnect an integration or request account deletion by contacting support.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@nudge.app.

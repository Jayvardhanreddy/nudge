Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2024

At Comment2DM, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number (optional), and password (hashed with bcrypt)
  • Instagram Data: Instagram account username, profile picture, follower count, and long-lived access tokens (encrypted with AES-256-GCM). Comment data and DM recipient information needed to process automations
  • Automation Events: Comments received, DMs sent, automation success/failure status, and timestamps
  • Billing Data: Payment records processed through Razorpay. We do not store card numbers — only Razorpay order IDs and payment IDs
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and general usage patterns for improving the service

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the Comment2DM automation service
  • To process DMs and comment replies on your behalf via Instagram's API
  • To display analytics and performance data on your dashboard
  • To process subscription payments via Razorpay
  • To send transactional emails (account creation, payment receipts, alerts)
  • To improve our service through aggregated, anonymized usage analytics

3. Data Retention

  • Automation events (DM logs): retained for 30 days, then automatically deleted via TTL indexes
  • Webhook events (duplicate check cache): retained for 7 days
  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • Payment records: retained for 7 years as required by tax law

4. Third-Party Services

We share data with trusted third parties only as necessary to provide the service:

  • Meta / Instagram: We use Instagram's Graph API to process comments and send DMs. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay: Payment processing. Subject to Razorpay's Privacy Policy
  • MongoDB Atlas: Secure cloud database hosting for user and automation data
  • Google OAuth: Optional sign-in via Google. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including: AES-256-GCM encryption for Instagram tokens, bcrypt password hashing, HTTPS-only connections, HTTP-only secure session cookies, and strict Content Security Policies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and download your personal data (contact us at privacy@comment2dm.com)
  • Correct inaccurate data via Settings → Profile
  • Delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Danger Zone
  • Withdraw consent for Instagram automation by disconnecting your Instagram account

7. Cookies

We use HTTP-only session cookies for authentication (named comment2dm_session). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No third-party analytics cookies are used.

8. Children's Privacy

Comment2DM is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notice.

10. Contact

For privacy-related requests or questions: privacy@comment2dm.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Nudge is an Instagram automation service. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact nudge.support360@gmail.com.

2. Personal data we collect

Depending on how you use Nudge, we may collect: your name and email address; account credentials needed to authenticate your Nudge account (passwords are stored only as one-way password hashes); your Nudge account identifiers; connected Instagram account identifiers and public profile information made available through Meta's official APIs; encrypted integration access tokens and their expiry information; automation settings such as keywords and reply text; support messages you send us; and technical information such as IP address, request timestamps, device/browser information and security logs.

3. Data from Instagram

When you connect Instagram, authorization occurs through Meta's official OAuth flow. Nudge does not ask for or store your Instagram password and does not use credential scraping. We access only data and permissions needed for the features you authorize.

4. Why we use data

We use personal data to create and authenticate accounts, connect and operate authorized Instagram integrations, run automations, provide analytics and service functionality, prevent abuse and protect security, troubleshoot failures, respond to support requests, maintain service records, and comply with applicable law.

5. Legal basis and consent

Where applicable, we process data to perform the service you request, based on your consent, for legitimate security and service operations, or to comply with legal obligations. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data to service providers that help us host, store, secure, monitor or operate Nudge, and to Meta where required to provide the Instagram integration. Providers receive only information reasonably needed for their service and are expected to protect it. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety and security.

7. International processing

Nudge and its service providers may process data in countries outside India. Where required, we use appropriate contractual, organizational or other safeguards and provide information about material changes to our processing practices.

8. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account operation, security, dispute handling and legal obligations. Connected Instagram data can be disconnected from your account. Security and technical logs may be retained for the periods required by applicable law.

9. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, update, delete or explain our handling of your personal data, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a grievance. Send requests to nudge.support360@gmail.com with enough information for us to identify the relevant account. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

10. Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your Nudge account and associated personal data, email nudge.support360@gmail.com. We will process the request subject to legal, security and legitimate operational retention requirements. Disconnecting Instagram does not by itself delete your Nudge account.

11. Cookies and local storage

Nudge may use browser cookies required for authentication and security. The website may also use browser local storage for optional creator-tool settings and preferences. These technologies are not a substitute for authentication and should not be used to store secrets.

12. Security

We use measures appropriate to the data and service, including HTTPS in production, bcrypt password hashing, encrypted storage for integration tokens, secure HTTP-only authentication cookies, access controls, rate limiting and security headers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's privacy

Nudge is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal data unlawfully, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as Nudge, applicable law or our processing practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

15. Contact and grievance

For privacy questions, data requests or grievances, contact nudge.support360@gmail.com. We will review and respond to requests within the period required by applicable law.

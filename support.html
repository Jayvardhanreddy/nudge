Help & Support

Use this page for account access, Instagram connection and automation issues, billing questions, privacy requests, or security reports.

Before production use: the operator must verify that the published support and privacy mailboxes are active, monitored, and able to handle requests within applicable legal deadlines. A web page alone does not create a functioning support process.

Login and account security

Use the password-recovery flow if available. Never share your password, session cookie, API key, Instagram access token, or reset link. If you suspect account compromise, disconnect affected integrations and contact support through the published channel.

Instagram automation

Confirm the connected Instagram account is eligible for the Meta API features used by this service, that the required permissions are approved, and that webhook subscriptions and access tokens are valid. Automation cannot bypass Meta platform rules, user consent, messaging restrictions, or rate limits.

Privacy and account deletion

Use Settings → Danger Zone to request account deletion. Deletion removes account-associated application records according to the retention policy; payment, fraud-prevention, legal records, backups, or provider-side copies may require separate retention or deletion handling where lawfully required. For access, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal, or grievance requests, use the privacy contact in the Privacy Policy.

Billing and refunds

Review the Refund Policy and Terms. Never send full card details or banking passwords to support.

Report a security issue

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through the published support/privacy channel. Do not publish tokens, personal data, exploit details, or database contents. The operator should acknowledge, assess, contain, patch and document incidents and make any notifications required by applicable law.

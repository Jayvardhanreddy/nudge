# Privacy Operations — Owner Checklist

This checklist is a deployment requirement, not evidence that every item has already been completed.

- [ ] Identify the legal entity/operator and publish a truthful, deployment-specific privacy notice.
- [ ] Inventory personal data, purposes, legal basis/consent, recipients/processors, locations and retention for each data flow.
- [ ] Provide usable access, correction, erasure, consent-withdrawal and grievance channels where required by applicable law.
- [ ] Verify requesters safely and propagate approved requests to databases, backups and processors according to documented retention rules.
- [ ] Review provider terms, subprocessors, data transfer and retention settings; disclose them accurately.
- [ ] For child-related processing, assess the specific legal requirements and exemptions; implement required verifiable parental consent and safeguards.
- [ ] Maintain access controls, audit logs, backup/restore tests, incident runbooks and a breach-notification decision process.
- [ ] Review applicable laws and commencement dates with qualified counsel before public launch; update the policy when data flows change.
- [ ] Test the privacy and support contact channels and keep them monitored.
- [ ] Re-audit after every material change to authentication, data models, AI providers, payments or integrations.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Free plan

The Nudge free plan does not require a paid subscription or payment method.

2. Paid subscriptions

When paid subscriptions are available, the applicable plan price, billing interval, taxes where applicable and material purchase terms are shown before payment. A subscription is created only after the payment flow is completed successfully.

3. Cancellation

You may request cancellation of a paid subscription by using the cancellation option provided in Nudge, where available, or by contacting nudge.support360@gmail.com. Cancellation stops future renewals according to the terms shown at checkout. Access during a paid billing period may continue according to the applicable plan terms.

4. Refund requests

If you were charged incorrectly, charged after a valid cancellation, or believe a payment requires review, contact nudge.support360@gmail.com promptly. Include the transaction or subscription reference supplied by the payment provider. We will review the request under the terms presented for the relevant purchase and applicable law.

5. Payment provider

Payments may be processed by a third-party payment provider. Payment credentials such as card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, OTPs and bank passwords should be entered only in the payment provider's secure checkout and should never be sent to Nudge support.

6. Questions

For billing questions, contact nudge.support360@gmail.com. See the Refunds and cancellations section of our Terms of Service as well.

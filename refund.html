Refund Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2024

We want you to be completely satisfied with Comment2DM. If you're not happy with your subscription, here's our refund policy.

📋 Summary: Refunds are available within 7 days of your first payment. No refunds for renewals or partial months.

Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • It is within 7 days of your first subscription payment for a given plan
  • You have not excessively used the service (i.e., sent more than 100 DMs using the paid plan features)
  • Your account is in good standing (not suspended for violations)

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Subscription renewals (monthly or annual auto-renewals)
  • Partial months — we do not prorate refunds for cancelled subscriptions mid-cycle
  • Accounts suspended for violations of our Terms of Service
  • Requests made after the 7-day window
  • Downgrades from a paid plan to a lower-tier plan

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at billing@comment2dm.com with:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your Razorpay Order ID (found in your Billing → Payment History)
  • Brief reason for the refund request

We will respond within 2 business days to confirm or deny your request.

Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days and returned to your original payment method. Bank processing times may vary.

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Cancellation stops future charges. You retain access to paid features until the end of your current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time in a billing cycle.

Contact

Billing questions: billing@comment2dm.com
